How flagging works
Good

CTR & viewability are at or above the benchmark for where the line ran.

Watch

80–99% of the benchmark — a little soft; worth a look at optimisation.

Needs attention

Below 80% of the benchmark, or a video completing under 20% (broken / mis-tracked).

A flagged line gets a red left bar + #FFE9ED tint. Marking it “looked into” calms the tint, but the bar stays until the metric recovers.

CTR is benchmarked against each publication’s own inventory (leave-one-out), not the estate average; viewability against the section average. Only lines with ≥2,000 impressions on a live booking (delivering / paused / ready) can be flagged.
